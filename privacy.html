Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Snake Game does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network requests to any server.

The app saves your settings (sound on/off, background theme and snake colour) only on your own device, using local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

Children: the app contains no ads, purchases or links, and collects nothing from anyone, including children.
