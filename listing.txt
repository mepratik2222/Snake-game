App name: Snake Game
Short description (80 chars): Classic snake game with swipe and on-screen key controls.
Full description:
Play the classic snake game on your phone.

• Control the snake with a swipe or with on-screen keys.
• Eat different fruits to grow; the snake changes colour as it grows.
• Settings: sound on/off, background theme, plain or dotted snake and colour choice, fruit mode.
• Pause any time. No ads, no sign-in, works offline.
